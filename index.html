IQBAL GOLDON TRADING CO.

ABOUTCOMPANY

IQBAL GOLDON TRADING CO. is a leading player in the scrap trading industry, committed to delivering sustainable and efficient solutions for scrap metal recycling and trading. With a robust network, professional operations, and a dedicated team operating from Yard No.A79 on Jeddah-Al Laith Road, we strive to revolutionize the scrap trading landscape while contributing to environmental conservation.

We take pride in introducing ourselves as one of the fast growing and the most trusted business partners of affluent corporate companies where we were able to demonstrate our business expertise in the shortest possible time.

We are known for our experience in disposal of scrap and dismantling and demolition work and client satisfaction. Our consultative, specialized and specified execution practices enable us in speeding up the disposal process as per our client requirement.

We are in scrap management & contracting business and our scrap business is a professionally managed organization providing one of the most certified scrap disposal services to various corporate clients within the Kingdom. Commercial, Industrial and Housing Residential Segments of Saudi Arabia.
